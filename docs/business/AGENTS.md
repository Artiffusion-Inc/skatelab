# Business Documentation

Business knowledge base covers product, market, competitors, finance, legal/IP, GTM, technology risks, team, and roadmap.

## Rules

- Distinguish measured facts from assumptions and estimates.
- Pricing, WTP, TAM/SAM/SOM, CAC, LTV, and forecasts require source, date, method, and confidence.
- CustDev claims include sample size and segment; anonymize respondents.
- Never convert training cost into product WTP without evidence.
- Keep hardware BOM and expenses linked to source records.
- Reconcile conflicting numbers across documents; do not silently select preferred values.
- Russian is default language for market-facing material.
