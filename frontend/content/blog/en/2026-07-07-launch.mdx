SkateLab explores figure skating review using video and movement data. The goal is to help coaches connect an observation with a clear task for the next attempt.

Public mobile app downloads are not open yet. This site has no equipment store, payment flow or programme registration. Any joint evaluation needs a separate discussion.

Start with the [recording guide](/blog/en/recording-guide), explore the [review sequence](/how-it-works), or [describe your task](/contact). Do not send personal footage or children’s data in your first message.
