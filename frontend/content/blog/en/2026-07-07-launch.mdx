SkateLab is an app project for coaches and skaters. We are developing video-based jump review and ways to compare recordings with data from skate-mounted sensors.

The public app has not been released yet. This website offers a [review example](/how-it-works) and [recording guides](/blog/en/recording-guide).

For questions about development or collaboration, [contact the team](/contact).
